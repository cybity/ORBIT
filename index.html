DATA SOURCES

Public data, direct from the source.

Every lookup is a direct browser request to a CORS-enabled public API. Nothing is proxied through an ORBIT server.

01

DNS over HTTPS

Cloudflare DoH for A, AAAA, MX, TXT, NS, CNAME, CAA, SOA, and reverse PTR records.

cloudflare-dns.com
02

RDAP

Registration data for domains and IP networks via the RDAP bootstrap service.

rdap.org
03

Certificate transparency

Certificate search and subdomain discovery from public CT logs, with an automatic fallback provider.

crt.sh + CertSpotter
04

Internet exposure

Observed ports, hostnames, CPEs, tags, and known vulnerabilities for IPs.

internetdb.shodan.io
05

Identity surface

GitHub, GitLab, Keybase, Hacker News, dev.to, and Codeberg public profiles.

api.github.com + 5
06

Archive history

Closest Wayback Machine snapshot for a domain or exact URL.

archive.org

METHOD

Evidence over noise.

ORBIT is deliberately narrow. It collects public records, keeps the raw response attached to every finding, and labels slow or failed sources instead of hiding them. No AI guesses. No inferred “risk score.”

Authorize first

Only investigate assets you own or have written permission to assess.

02
